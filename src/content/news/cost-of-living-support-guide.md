I know many residents in Canning Town South are still feeling the squeeze from the cost of living. Here's a round-up of support that's currently available — please share it with anyone who might need it.

## Council Tax Reduction

If you're on a low income, you may be entitled to a reduction on your Council Tax bill. This isn't just for people on benefits — it's worth checking even if you're working. My office can help you make a claim or check an existing one.

## Household Support Fund

Newham Council distributes emergency support for food, energy, and essential costs through the Household Support Fund. Applications are usually made through the council's welfare rights team or via a local referral — get in touch if you're not sure where to start.

## Free debt and benefits advice

Local advice services can check you're getting everything you're entitled to, and help if you're struggling with debt or facing a benefit sanction or overpayment dispute.

## How I can help

A lot of this support isn't automatic — you often have to know it exists and apply for it. If you're not sure what you're entitled to, or a form or process is getting confusing, [contact my office](/contact/) and I'll help you find the right route, or push the council on your behalf if something's gone wrong.
