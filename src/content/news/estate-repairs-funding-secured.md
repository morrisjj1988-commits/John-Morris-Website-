After months of casework and resident meetings, I'm glad to confirm that Newham Council has agreed a dedicated repairs and damp-treatment programme for blocks on Hallsville Road.

This follows a series of reports from residents about damp, mould, and long-outstanding repair requests. I organised a walkabout with council housing officers in the spring, and have been pushing for a proper survey and funded programme ever since.

## What happens next

- Surveyors will visit affected blocks over the coming weeks to confirm the scope of works.
- Residents with an open repair request should have already been contacted; if you haven't heard anything by the end of the month, get in touch with my office.
- I'll be holding a residents' update meeting once a firm timetable is confirmed.

This is a good step forward, but I know it doesn't fix everything overnight. If you're dealing with a housing issue anywhere in Canning Town South — whether it's repairs, damp and mould, overcrowding, or a dispute with your landlord or housing association — [get in touch](/find-help/) and I'll do what I can to help.
