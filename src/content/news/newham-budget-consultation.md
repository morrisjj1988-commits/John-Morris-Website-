Newham Council's annual budget consultation is open, and I'd encourage every Canning Town South resident to have a look and respond, even briefly. This is genuinely one of the moments where resident feedback shapes real decisions about local services.

## What's being consulted on

The council is asking for views on spending priorities across housing services, street cleansing and environment, youth and community services, and adult social care, against a backdrop of continued pressure on council budgets nationally.

## Why it's worth five minutes

Budget consultations can feel abstract, but the responses genuinely feed into scrutiny discussions and, in my experience, do shift priorities — particularly on things like street cleansing frequency and community centre funding, which came up repeatedly in previous years' consultations.

## How to respond

The consultation is open on the council's website for a few more weeks. If you'd rather share your views with me directly and have me raise them at scrutiny, that works too — [get in touch](/contact/) and I'll make sure your points are heard.
