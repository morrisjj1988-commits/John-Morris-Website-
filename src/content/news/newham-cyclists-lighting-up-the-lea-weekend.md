My weekend kicked off with Newham Cyclists, leading a cycle tour around the north of the borough. It was a great opportunity to revisit several key infrastructure projects I delivered during my time on the Council, including the Westfield Avenue project, the Leyton Road CFR3, the Atherton LTN, modal filters on Keogh Road and McGrath Road, the Romford Road corridor, and the West Ham LTN. Along the route, we chatted with local children and residents about their everyday experiences cycling in Newham and why safe, connected infrastructure is so vital.

## Lighting Up the Lea at Cody Dock

Next, I headed over to Cody Dock for the Lighting Up the Lea festival. The event featured a vibrant mix of workshops and activities, but the highlight for me was a deep dive into local history. The site where the Brunel Works now stands in Canning Town was once home to Thames Ironworks — the last major shipyard in London. It built pioneering ironclad warships, including HMS Warrior. Remarkably, its owner, Arnold Hills, not only founded West Ham United, but was also the Chair of the Vegetarian Society. His work proved invaluable when Mahatma Gandhi visited London and struggled to find vegetarian meals!

## The People's Emergency Briefing

Afterward, I dropped by the People's Emergency Briefing. The session highlighted the sheer scale of the climate crisis, showcasing both the critical challenges we face and the practical actions we can take to address them. Seeing so many dedicated activists across Newham coming together with such passion for climate action was deeply inspiring.

![A speaker presenting "The People's Emergency Briefing" on a screen to a seated audience](./newham-cyclists-lighting-up-the-lea-weekend-emergency-briefing.jpg)

## Volunteering at the London Cycling Festival

The following day, I joined Newham Cyclists once again — this time volunteering for the London Cycling Festival. Our group led a ride starting in East Ham, swinging through West Ham to pick up more riders, and heading onward to Victoria Park. After spending time exploring the festival stalls and community stalls, we made the scenic ride back along the same route to wrap up an inspiring, action-packed weekend.
