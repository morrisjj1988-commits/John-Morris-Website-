Seb Dance, Deputy Mayor for Transport, has replied to my request for speed cameras on Silvertown Way and Peto Street North. There's still no timescale for new fixed cameras, but the reply points to a faster route: a council referral that can get mobile speed cameras out within about two weeks.

## What I Asked For

In July, [I wrote to Kaya Comer-Schwartz](/news/road-safety-is-my-priority/), Deputy Mayor for Policing and Crime, copying in Seb Dance. I formally requested speed enforcement cameras on Silvertown Way and Peto Street North.

I asked for three things: cameras on both roads, a clear answer on what action could be taken and when, and a site visit or joint meeting with TfL, the Met's Roads and Transport Policing Command and Newham Council officers.

My letter was passed to the Deputy Mayor for Transport, and his reply arrived on 1 October.

## What the Reply Says

The Deputy Mayor restates City Hall's commitment to Vision Zero. The second Vision Zero Action Plan (VZAP2) includes an action to expand the safety camera network on both TfL roads and borough roads.

On **fixed cameras**, TfL installed trial cameras at 10 locations earlier this year. It is also building capacity with the Met to enforce up to one million offences a year, and re-letting its camera contracts. Once that work is done, TfL says it will choose new camera sites based on risk, in consultation with councils.

On **mobile cameras**, TfL has given PCSOs in its Road Danger Reduction Team a bigger role in speed enforcement. That means more mobile cameras at peak times, in the evenings and at weekends.

The most useful part is the **London Borough Speed Referral** process. Council officers can ask TfL and the Met to send mobile speed cameras where residents have raised concerns. The Deputy Mayor says cameras typically go out within two weeks if the referral includes enough evidence.

## What Happens Next

Mobile cameras aren't a permanent fix, but they could start changing driver behaviour within weeks. So I'll be asking Newham's officers to submit a London Borough Speed Referral for Silvertown Way and Peto Street North, backed by the evidence residents have shared with me. On Peto Street North, I'll keep pressing the Council for engineering measures too.

I'll also write back to the Deputy Mayor. I'll ask for Silvertown Way to be assessed as one of the new VZAP2 camera locations, and for a clear answer on the joint site visit.

Your experiences make the referral stronger. If you've had a near-miss or seen dangerous driving on these roads, please [sign the petition](/road-safety-campaign/#sign-the-petition) and tell me what happened. I'll keep you updated on what comes back.

*— Cllr John Morris*
