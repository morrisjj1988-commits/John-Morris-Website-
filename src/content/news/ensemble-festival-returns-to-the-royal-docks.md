This weekend, Royal Victoria Dock trades its usual waterside calm for two days of circus, dance, and street theatre as Ensemble Festival returns this summer, transforming Royal Victoria into a vibrant outdoor stage for contemporary performance.

Best of all, it's a free festival bringing together bold, joyful and thought-provoking circus, dance and theatre, running Saturday 25 and Sunday 26 July, 12:00-20:00, at Royal Victoria Dock, Western Gateway.

## What's on the bill

The programme leans heavily into physical performance and spectacle:

- **Fragments of Us** by Talawa Theatre, in collaboration with FUBUNATION and Sonia Hughes, is a dance and spoken word performance exploring ancestry, belonging and shared experiences.
- **Garbh** by Shyam Dattani and Mira Salat offers a contemporary reimagining of the Gujarati folk dance Garba.
- **ElevateHer** by Daughters of the Wire is an all-female wire walking performance celebrating female camaraderie and empowerment.
- **The Torch** by Nigel "Kobby" Taylor blends Afrobeat, hip-hop, rap and storytelling into a gig-theatre experience.
- **Stick and Stone** by Ferdinando + Bernstein is a physical comedy performance using striking visual imagery.
- **Balance** by Brainfools is a circus-theatre show combining found objects and acrobatics, reflecting on pub culture.
- **Captain C-Man** by Cormac Mohally brings a juggling and circus spectacle for all ages.

There's also an international flavour this year: Certain Blacks are showcasing performances from some of the most exciting artists from Catalonia, including a piece exploring water scarcity through poetic storytelling, a clown suspended over a public street, and a street circus duet inspired by mythology.

Most acts perform twice a day, so there's flexibility to catch favourites at different points across the afternoon and evening — though timings are subject to change, so it's worth checking the full programme before heading down.

## While you're there

Round out the day with the IFS Cloud Cable Car for panoramic river views, or grab a bite at ZeroSette, an Italian spot near the ExCeL.

Tickets aren't required — it's a free, all-ages event, easy to drop into for an hour or stay for the whole afternoon.
