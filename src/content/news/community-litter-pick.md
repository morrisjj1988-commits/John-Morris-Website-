A brilliant turnout on Saturday for our monthly community litter pick — over thirty residents came along to help clear Butchers Road and the footpaths near the Lower Lea Crossing.

Thank you to everyone who gave up their Saturday morning, and to the local scout group who brought the whole troop along. Between us we filled more than twenty bags, which Newham Council's cleansing team collected the same afternoon.

These sessions are as much about community as they are about litter — it's a great chance to meet neighbours and talk about what else is going on locally. If you'd like to get involved with the next one, or you know a spot that needs particular attention, drop me a message.

**Next litter pick:** first Saturday of next month, meeting at 10am outside Canning Town Library. All welcome, equipment provided.
