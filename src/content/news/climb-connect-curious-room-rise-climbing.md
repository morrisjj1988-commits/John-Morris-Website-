This Saturday, 5 September, The Curious Room is taking its social club to the wall for a morning of climbing, conversation, and a founder Q&A at Rise Climbing.

The Curious Room is a London social club built on one idea: the most interesting thing in any room is the people in it. Join them at Rise Climbing for a founder Q&A (ask them anything, and they'll ask you back), group climbing, and the kind of conversations that happen naturally when you're not stuck holding a name tag in a corner.

## What to expect

- Talk and open Q&A with Rise Climbing's founders
- Climbing for all levels, beginners very welcome
- Connect with other curious minds

Shoes are provided, so just wear something you're comfortable moving in. Spaces are limited, so see you on the wall!
