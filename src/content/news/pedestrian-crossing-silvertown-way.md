Good news for anyone who regularly crosses Silvertown Way near the retail park: Newham Council's highways team has agreed to install a new signalled pedestrian crossing at the junction, following a safety campaign led by local parents and residents.

I raised this directly with the Cabinet Member for Environment and Transport after being contacted by a group of parents whose children cross here on the school run, along with several near-miss reports from residents over the last year.

## Timeline

- Detailed design and utility checks: this summer
- Construction: expected in the autumn, subject to final sign-off
- I'll share exact dates as soon as the council confirms them

Road safety is one of the issues I hear about most from residents in Canning Town South, particularly around school routes. If there's a crossing, junction, or stretch of road in the ward you think needs attention, let me know — these campaigns work best when residents and their councillor push for change together.
